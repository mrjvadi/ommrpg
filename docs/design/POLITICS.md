# POLITICS.md

## Political Layers
- player
- city
- kingdom
- world
- Sacred Land

## Treaties
Support:
- alliance
- peace
- non-aggression
- mutual defense
- trade
- research
- resource agreements

## Embargo
Kingdoms may stop trading with others.
This affects resource availability, reputation and prices.

## City Politics
Mayor/owner can define:
- entry policy
- fees
- master access
- taxes
- residency
- public facility access

## Diplomacy
Diplomats negotiate contracts, treaties, access and master exchanges.

## Espionage
Players/AI can scout and gather information.
All results are server-authoritative.

## Sacred Land
Neutral.
No normal Kingdom warfare.
Diplomacy, trade, teaching, research and contracts are active.
