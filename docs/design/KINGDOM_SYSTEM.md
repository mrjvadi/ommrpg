# KINGDOM_SYSTEM.md

## Kingdom
Players can found a Kingdom: a guild with members, treasury and territory.

## Kingdom State
- leader
- council
- members
- treasury
- territory
- resources
- buildings
- military
- reputation
- alliances
- wars
- treaties
- knowledge

## Membership
A player may belong to multiple Kingdoms.
Membership is per Kingdom.

## Roles
Examples:
- King
- General
- Treasurer
- Researcher
- Diplomat
- Resource Minister
- Commander

## Buildings
- Castle
- Barracks
- Alchemy Lab
- Forge
- Formation Hall
- Farm
- Mine
- Bank
- Academy
- Research Center
- Market
- Warehouse
- Teleport Gate

## Shared Knowledge
Kingdoms may create academies, research programs and shared knowledge pools.

## Kingdom Economy
Kingdoms can:
- produce
- trade
- tax
- invest
- build
- finance wars
- control resources

## Kingdom Power
Power is not simply military strength.
Knowledge, trade, resources, gates, population and diplomacy all matter.
