A player may:
- buy a city
- found a city
- restore an abandoned city
- inherit a city
- capture a city where rules allow

## Masters
Masters belong to or contract with cities.

A player searching for a specific master in another city may need:
- entry fee
- mayor permission
- reputation
- contract
- citizenship
- special permit

## Master Exchange
Cities can negotiate temporary master contracts.

Example:
City A pays City B for a master's 7-day teaching residency.
