# CREATION_SYSTEM.md

## Purpose
Procedurally create:
- items
- weapons
- armor
- potions
- artifacts
- spells
- formations
- creatures
- soldiers
- constructs

## Inputs
- knowledge
- mastery
- class
- profession
- energy
- materials
- root
- hidden talent
- equipment
- environment
- world
- event
- specialist
- operation order
- seeded randomness

## Known Crafting
A known recipe provides constrained outcomes.

## Experimental Crafting
A player may combine materials, knowledge and actions without a complete known recipe.

Possible outcomes:
- success
- failure
- mutation
- discovery
- new recipe
- new knowledge
- unique item
- unexpected side effect

## Specialists
Some items require a specialist.

Example:
An ancient weapon may require:
- blacksmith
- formation master
- soul specialist
- alchemist

## Quality
Final quality can depend on:
- specialist mastery
- material quality
- root compatibility
- tool quality
- energy
- environment
- sequence
- recipe understanding

## Procedural Soldiers
Example:
Necromancer + Death Knowledge + Soul Energy + Corpse + Ritual -> Undead Soldier.

Demonic knowledge + contract + blood + spiritual energy -> Demon.

Beast knowledge + life energy + nature affinity -> Tamed Beast.

## Unit Evolution
Units can gain:
- experience
- mastery
- traits
- abilities
- evolution paths
- loyalty

## Fusion
Support compatible:
- item fusion
- knowledge fusion
- class evolution
- material fusion

Fusion outcomes must follow world rules.

## Identity
Unique creations track:
- creator
- timestamp
- location
- inputs
- method
- lineage
- history

## Anti-Exploit
All creation is server-side.
Validate materials and specialist requirements.
Prevent duplicate unique creations and replay.
