# TRADE.md

## Trade Modes
- direct trade
- market
- auction
- bulk order
- contract
- caravan
- cross-world shipment

## Contracts
Support:
- parties
- goods
- quantity
- frequency
- duration
- price
- collateral
- breach rules
- cancellation rules

## Trade Routes
Trade can be exposed to:
- attack
- blockade
- taxation
- gate failure
- political embargo

## Master Contracts
A city may hire a master from another city for:
- training
- research
- temporary residency
- academy sessions

## Interdependence
Kingdoms and cities should specialize in different resources and services.

## Security
Trade settlement must be transactional and idempotent.
