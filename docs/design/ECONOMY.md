# ECONOMY.md

## Economic Layers
### Game Economy
Gold, food, wood, iron, stone, crystals, herbs and world-specific materials.

### Knowledge Economy
Knowledge, recipes, techniques, teaching, licensing, academies and discoveries.

### Real-Money Layer
Optional premium currency (cosmetics and convenience only) and optional blockchain assets.

## Principle
Never sell direct overwhelming power.

## Sources
- quests
- exploration
- production
- trade
- crafting
- events
- taxes
- city services

## Sinks
- crafting
- construction
- maintenance
- taxes
- research
- training
- gate fees
- city services
- market fees
- war logistics

## Economy Integrity
All authoritative balances are transactional and auditable in PostgreSQL.
Use an append-only logical ledger.

## Scarcity
Resources may be:
- renewable
- finite
- rare
- region-specific
- world-specific
- event-only
- hidden

## City Economy
Cities can earn from:
- markets
- academies
- masters
- crafting
- production
- teleport gates
- licensing

Cities pay:
- maintenance
- staff
- masters
- energy
- security
- taxes

## Premium
Premium should focus on:
- cosmetics
- convenience
- presentation
- optional services
- social features
not raw power.

## Marketplace
Support listings, purchases, auctions, bulk orders and contracts.
Fees are configurable.
