# KNOWLEDGE_SYSTEM.md

## Core Rule
Knowledge, Understanding and Mastery are separate.

Example:
Knowledge 100%
Understanding 40%
Mastery 2%

## Knowledge Data
Each knowledge object may have:
- domain
- concepts
- rarity
- complexity
- compatibility
- prerequisites
- knowledge percent
- understanding percent
- mastery percent
- origin
- lineage
- version
- hidden properties

## Learning
Sources:
- teachers
- books
- practice
- exploration
- combat
- research
- experimentation
- trade
- licenses
- academies

## Mastery
Mastery grows through practice and actual use.
It belongs to the character.

## Teaching
Masters may teach knowledge and understanding.
Every master has:
- knowledge
- mastery
- teaching skill
- capacity
- specialization
- personality
- availability

## Transfer
Supported mechanisms:
- direct teaching
- scrolls
- recipes
- licenses
- academy training
- kingdom knowledge

## Hidden Information
Knowledge can reveal properties gradually.
Unknown information may appear as ??? until a character has enough understanding.

## Evolution
Highly mastered knowledge can evolve into variants.
Keep lineage so the history of an evolved technique is preserved.

## World First
The first discoverer receives permanent historical credit.
Ownership may later change without changing first-discoverer history.
